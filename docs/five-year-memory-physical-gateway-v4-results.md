# Completed physical/Gateway V4: raw recall succeeds; writer delivery remains weak

The actual production Store/Retriever/Gateway completed the frozen six-year corpus and all **432 checkpoint cases per arm**. The raw arm delivered every required original at the final checkpoint: **144/144 cases, 396/396 exact original records, and 12/12 rare raw-only details**. All retained originals passed their recorded hash/text checks; six explicitly requested message originals were physically removed and verified absent. This is successful measured source delivery, **not a passed final model-answer gate**.

| Matched preprocessing | First year | Third year | Final five-plus-year source delivery | Final originals |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw originals | 144/144 | 144/144 | 144/144 | 396/396 |
| Same independent writer notes | 115/144 | 113/144 | 113/144 | 227/396 |

The same writer arm still lost relevant originals as notes accumulated. Rare raw detail delivery was 8/12, and cross-entity hybrid evidence consumed the limited context before some wanted originals. Against its matched f2 writer baseline, the complete-source-delivery error ratio was **0.939**, so this metric did not halve. The raw pair improved from 130/144 to 144/144, an error ratio of zero **for source delivery only**. Grounded answer correctness, range completion by a reader, and any twofold answer-quality claim remain unmeasured in these artifacts.

Actual managed-local privacy replay passed in both arms. Twelve cached first-year prepared scopes, all of which had once contained the protected values, were rejected after policy changed. An actual first-year streaming backup was imported into the existing installation, current policy survived unchanged, and six deletion jobs re-purged restored originals with zero pending jobs. All twelve new restored queries completed, with zero blocks and zero protected-value exposures in candidates/context. This checks newly served output and local persistence; it does not erase previously delivered bytes, RAM copies, provider archives, external backups, untracked working copies, or policy after a fresh installation/data clear.

The corpus contains 2,191 synthetic days, 184,044 daily distractors and 408 injected original definitions. After six verified deletions, the canonical copy has 402 retained originals, 184,428 messages, 18 device events and an equal 184,428-row message FTS index. The writer copy contains 180 accepted notes; twelve raw-only notes were rejected exactly as in the comparator. Both SQLite integrity checks return `ok`. The raw database is 136,089,600 bytes; the writer database is 136,708,096 bytes. All first-year source dates are at least five whole calendar years before the synthetic final date; five real years of deployment did not elapse.

Final native preparation p50/p95 was **5,818/13,103 ms** for raw and **5,851/12,974 ms** for writer on the shared host. These times exclude model reasoning, phone UI, and Work execution. The observed full-table ranking cost and writer packing failures motivated a new independently tested indexed/specificity Gateway. That later version must be measured in its own immutable phase; V4 cannot inherit its latency or quality results.

The writer used source-only originals but saw the complete timeline together. Notes were replayed only after all their referenced dates. This prevents literal future references at earlier checkpoints, but does not establish a causal incremental extractor or future-blind wording. The harness records those limits explicitly.

All actual scoring rows, final blinded packets, exact fixture and production manifests, raw byte hashes, counts, replay receipts and logs are retained in [the V4 artifacts](benchmarks/five-year-memory-physical-gateway-v4/paired-summary.json). Large scoring rows and packet files use deterministic gzip compression. Original native corpus and source-only reader state are under `/tmp/hoyoung-five-year-final-gateway-{raw,writer}-v4`; the independent [native reader tools](five-year-native-reader-tools.md) can now use those exact copies. The matrix and writer hashes, corpus construction, questions, source limits and 12,000-character budget were unchanged. Paid API calls and actual Work/physical-phone calls were zero.

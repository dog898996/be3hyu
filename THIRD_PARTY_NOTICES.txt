The Android browser connection vendors the unmodified DOM module from
Chat On Steroids 2.1.37, commit 2d1c86efed698ed759e17efded4e12ca2d78b28e,
https://github.com/totec448-spec/chat-on-steroids. The app uses its composer,
Send/Stop and busy observation. Its desktop application, MCP
server, browser extension service worker and provider React reader are not bundled.
Source and checksum: mobile/app/src/main/assets/vendor/chat-on-steroids/source.json
